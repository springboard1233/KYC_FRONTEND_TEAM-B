Welcome to SmartKYC

KYC verification with fraud detection ensures that identities are accurately verified while detecting and preventing potential fraudulent activities.

Start Verification
📄

Step 1: Upload Your KYC

Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Driving Licence

✅

Step 2: Submit for Verification

Click Submit after uploading documents.

🔍

Step 3: Extract Your Details from KYC

System extracts details from documents (OCR).

Step 4: Fraud Analysis

  • ✅ Low Risk → Genuine
  • ⚠️ Medium Risk → Needs manual review
  • ❌ High Risk → Likely fraud
📊

Step 5: Receive Verification Result

  • ✅ Verified / Approved
  • ❌ Rejected
  • ⏳ Pending / Under Review
  • ⚠️ Fraud Suspected
🔄

Step 6: Retry or Appeal (if needed)

  • 📤 Re-upload documents
  • ✏️ Correct mistakes
  • ✅ Resubmit for verification
🎉

Step 7: Completion

  • 🔑 Access services that require KYC
  • 📄 View verification report
  • 🗂️ Status stored for audit/compliance
